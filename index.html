About Us

Novel Solutions Corporation is a Philippine-based engineering and technical services company dedicated to maximizing the performance, reliability, and lifecycle of critical assets in the semiconductor and electronics industries.

Every engagement is customized around your specific equipment, technical requirements, agreed scope of work, and strict acceptance criteria. Rooted in the Philippines, NOVEL SOLUTIONS CORPORATION is committed to quality-minded execution and continuous capability development—building enduring partnerships that drive long-term operational value

OUR MISSION

Engineering that moves ideas forward.

To maximize the reliability and service life of critical semiconductor and electronic assets through disciplined engineering, quality craftsmanship, and practical lifecycle solutions—empowering our partners to operate with total confidence.

OUR VISION

A trusted partner.

To become a respected Philippine-based technical services partner in the global semiconductor and electronics ecosystem, recognized for engineering capability, service reliability, customer partnership, and a sustained commitment to improvement.

WHAT WE DO

Keep your equipment
running at its best.

From full-system refurbishment to board-level repairs, get practical engineering support that helps your critical equipment perform reliably.

Semiconductor Equipment Refurbishment & Reconditioning

Give essential equipment a fresh start. We assess, rebuild, recondition, and repair systems and assemblies—then inspect, adjust, and functionally test them to help restore reliable operation.

Equipment Assembly, Upgrading & Modification

Build for what’s next. We assemble equipment and modules, upgrade systems, and support modifications and integration around your configuration, specifications, and project needs.

Equipment Testing, Inspection & Post-Test Operations

Know it’s ready to run. Our functional checks, troubleshooting, and performance validation are backed by documented results and acceptance criteria tailored to your specifications.

PCB & Board-Level Electronics Repair

Get to the root of board-level issues. We assess, troubleshoot, repair, and refurbish printed circuit boards and electronic assemblies, with post-repair checks where applicable.
