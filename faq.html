What does Novel Solutions specialize in?

Novel Solutions Corporation provides semiconductor test engineering and technical services, including device testing, engineering support, yield and data analytics, and test automation.

What is semiconductor test engineering?

Test engineering develops and applies methods to evaluate semiconductor devices. It helps verify functionality and performance, identify issues, and provide useful information for product and manufacturing teams.

What is the difference between wafer and final testing?

Wafer testing evaluates individual dies before they are packaged. Final testing evaluates packaged devices after assembly. Both stages help identify defects and confirm device performance.

Can you support test data and yield analysis?

Yes. Test data analysis can help identify failure patterns, understand yield behavior, and support engineering decisions. The specific scope depends on your devices, data, and objectives.

Do you provide automation and software support?

We support software and automation efforts that improve testing productivity, data accuracy, workflow repeatability, and traceability.

How do I start a conversation about a project?
